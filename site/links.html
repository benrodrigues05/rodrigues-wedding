Invitation links

One link per couple, family or person. Their names go on the envelope, and their RSVP only lets them choose up to the number of people you put here.

One invitation per line: the names as they should appear on the envelope, a comma, then how many people it’s for. The list is remembered on this device.

{name} and {link} are filled in for each invitation. You can still edit it in WhatsApp before sending.

How to send them

  1. Scroll to an invitation below and tap Send on WhatsApp.
  2. WhatsApp opens with the message and their link. Pick the person or group and send.
  3. Come back here. That invitation is ticked as sent; carry on with the next.

For SMS, email or Instagram, tap Copy link and paste it in. Copy all links copies the whole list to paste into Notes or a spreadsheet.

Copy it yourself

Your browser didn’t allow copying. Press and hold the text below, choose Select All, then Copy.

    Your links will appear here.
