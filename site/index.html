No need to buy a whole new outfit. If your best dress or suit isn’t in these shades, add a splash of colour instead: a bright tie or bow tie with a dark suit, or a hat, fascinator or scarf that matches the palette.

Getting There

Collisheen Estate is on Esenembi Road near Umhlali, just inland from Ballito. It’s about 20 minutes from King Shaka International Airport and 30 minutes from central Durban, and there’s plenty of parking at the venue.
